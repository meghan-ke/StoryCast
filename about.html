Every story deserves to be heard.

StoryCast is a space for stories that bring people closer, encourage understanding and remind us of the power of community.

Our purpose

Stories connect us.

Behind every story is an experience, a perspective and an opportunity to understand someone else's world. StoryCast brings these experiences together through video and accessible written content.

We believe stories about belonging, mental health and community can encourage empathy, open conversations and help people recognise the value of supporting one another.

Access for everyone

Experience stories your way.

StoryCast is designed to offer more than one way to engage with a story. We are working to make the experience clear, flexible and accessible.

Read along

Expandable transcripts provide a text alternative to spoken content so visitors can read at their own pace.

Watch with captions

Where available, use video captions to follow dialogue and meaningful audio information. We aim to select accessible media and check caption accuracy.

Navigate with confidence

Clear page structure, meaningful headings, keyboard focus indicators and responsive layouts help visitors move through the site.

Choose your experience

Stories are presented without automatic playback, allowing visitors to decide when to start a video.
