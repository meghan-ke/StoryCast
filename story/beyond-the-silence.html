Beyond the Silence

Finding Peace is noticing when the mind wanders and knowing how to bring it back to the present!

Community and belonging . Audio

The story

The strength of Self

In a world full of distractions, Janae Johnson invites listeners to pause and reconnect with themselves through mindfulness.

The meditation shows that finding peace isn't about stopping every thought; it's about noticing when the mind wanders and gently returning to the present.
